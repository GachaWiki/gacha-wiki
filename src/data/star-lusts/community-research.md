# Star Lusts community research (TW/JP/CN/EN)

Sweep done 2026-11-09 (rebuilt after the previous session died mid-research — this file
exists so it can't be lost again). Game title per community: 星欲少女 (JP/CN/TW all use
the kanji), 러스트 시스터즈 (KR), Star Lusts (EN). Server layout matters: the FANZA/DMM
version (JP, released 2024-02-05) runs on SEPARATE servers from Global; 仮想演算/Virtual

guaranteed-SSR tutorial reroll.

### Attacker meta (their own words, 2026-05)
- 星/Star: **ミア (UR)** strongest overall ("surpasses the star barrage slot", ignores
  element matchups; sig weapon + poison gear + drone). シャチ = star laser, strong but
  needs high dupes (UR+2 crit), ~80% of Mia at release γ. 化け猫ナナ permanent laser,
  cheap dupes, poor cost-performance but 300k in 禁足地攻撃VII. 低レアミア used once
  (Arena S12 second DPS).
- 虚空/Void: 悪夢アイリー dominant (top-13 Virtual Calc S5 all UR+5, ~213k). イブリース
  laser (sig gear +50% ATK under 70% energy + huge regen; SP is barrage → blocked by
  laser-jammer bosses). Machi (ウミウサギ field, 5x/sec, no crits/status in field).
  根性-laser (ATK +50% over time, clashes with switching). 初期キャラ Void laser support
  (homing missiles, 17% chance heal, full-heal SP, poison carries; shines 浄化の試練II).
- 物理/Physical: アイニー top pick ("simple and very usable", honey-charged piercing
  laser). 嫁アイニー self-sufficient 2nd physical. マギー (manual spirit gauge; used
  Arena S13 — partly because ラミ is 機工少女 so her material is scarce). Point-blank
  3-way barrage char (used S4 vs adds; rare off-field healer scaling with max HP).
- エーテル/Ether: essentially ONE ether attacker (laser; SP leaves laser in place while
  moving — bad vs mobile bosses).
- エクト/Ectoplasm: burn laser char (ATK+20% at 60% energy, burn+20% at 80%; poison gear
  on top of burn) preferred over ジェイ for simplicity. 純白モード barrage char (SP
  auto-tracking via MANDATORY sig gear; comparable to ミア; big gap vs クリオネ擬態ミー;
  UR+2 changes gauge mechanics).
- Rule of thumb: top scores need ≥1 barrage + ≥1 laser attacker PER ELEMENT (jammer
  bosses negate one bullet type).

### Supporter meta (2026-05; "no single correct answer")
- 複数効果系 (top): **アグネス** = 最強サポーター (Hot Wave field: enemy 虚弱 +50% taken
  AND ally crit +35% in one package; needs HP 37500+/ATK 5250+ and sig weapon).
  **イーネス** (back-row auto-attack every 5s; crit → party crit +5%, enemy crit-weak
  +20% crit dmg; theory 70%+ crit at UR; 50–70% with Agnes). **花嫁ヒルダ** (attack
  speed +40 field; SP = 2 fields, +50 speed / +60% final dmg; "unconditionally strong").
  **コニー** (energy-regen field; UR+2 required, UR+5 = 3s duration; SP gives all +40
  energy instantly).
- 虚弱系: 化け猫ナナ (10% always, up to 85% with sig gear; auto-track), 花嫁虚弱+15%
  char, サンシャイン系 shark-barrage (10–30%/10s, HP 30000 low bar), 蜘蛛の印 char
  (30%/9s + 解放線II switch-in +20% — pairs with ジェイ), random-buff char (UR+2:
  虚弱+30%).
- 控えDPS: はなりん (back-row barrage + physical field + ヒトデちゃん summon), 祈光
  char (ATK+20%, crit+40%, barrage summon; Ether so no weak element).
- Energy: 萌え萌えきゅんカーラ / 大切な恋人アリエル (+0.3/s with 2 special-type units).
- ATK-up系 explicitly deprioritized ("multiplicative buffs — dmg-taken-up, crit — win").
- Misc names: 攻速 = 新入社員マンディー, Let'sトレーニングカーラ, 孤独な人妻モリー;
  crit-aura = リオン 20%, 嫁ミア 18%, モリー 10%. 磁場 chars: ユナ, 沖田凜々花, カーラ,
  ハナビ, ジュン, アリエル, はなりん, デイジー, ムー, リウ.
- Math notes: ATK buffs additive; dmg-taken-up multiplicative; poison/corruption damage
  unaffected by ATK buffs (unverified); no crits on special attacks.

## CN — the most tier-list-minded community, but frozen in 2023–2024

- 9Game 進階攻略+強度榜 (2023-11-21, author 魏虎娃): https://www.9game.cn/news/9397199.html
  — cross-posted on cd6.com (https://www.cd6.com/gl/162080.html). Despite the title
  there is NO formal ladder; it's team comps (implicit strength proxy):
  * 至尊摆烂队: SSR银鸮 + SR铃牛 + UR八爪 (毒×3) — "very painful post-launch".
  * 老大队: 白熊/虎鲸/珊瑚 pick 2 (腐+毒+毒).
  * 老二队: 企鹅/地狱犬/腔棘鱼 pick 2 (腔棘鱼 shines after 超常解放b).
  * 如SSR队: 夜兔+水母+flex.
  * 三色队: 白熊+虎鲸+菜鸟 — "first top-tier comp since launch" (weak vs physical).
  * 虚空杀手队: 虎鲸+毒蝎+日光(+专武) (毒+毒+回能) — counters void bosses.
  * Substitutions: 秘书<地狱犬; 菜鸟=珊瑚; 毒蝎=虎鲸; 艳阳+专武≈UR珊瑚 else ≈水母;
    日光+专武<地狱犬.
  * Gear meta: first poison set, then 2nd; corruption for 白熊/八爪/企鹅; avoid 神火
    子机 with fire sets and 恶鬼 with poison sets; 白精灵 + 强袭 are the universal
    子机; batch-synthesize 6–8, lock poison gear, pulled 专武 auto-locks.
- 新手开荒流程攻略 (2023-12): reroll flow ~10 min (20 pulls beginner pool); "国家队" =
  原皮虎鲸 (Stellergy main DPS) + 白熊 (healer, 10s invincibility ult) + 海兔 (Void
  main DPS — collab-limited, NO LONGER OBTAINABLE).
- 星欲少女 T0角色有哪些 (cd6, 2024-04): T0 article exists, not yet fetched.
- GameKee hosts a 星欲少女 wiki but behind QQ login/verification — not scrapeable.
- Bilibili video guides (低氪金指南, 越級擊殺BOSS, activity mode intros) + a 交流群.

## Verdict for the wiki

JP (wagashi) is the only source current to 2026 and deep enough to anchor a
community/meta section. CN comps are 3 years stale (pre- UR/costume powercreep) but
historically interesting; EN/TW are thin. Server differences (DMM vs Global) must be
stated on any tier/meta page.
